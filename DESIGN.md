# Escape from School: Game Design

This file holds everything we have decided about the game. We add to it every time we decide something new.

## The idea

School is too boring, so you escape. The game is **funny**, not scary.

**The goal:** it is the end of term, and the whole school has to go to a super boring assembly. You must escape **before the assembly starts**. Your bike is waiting in the car park, so you need to get there and ride away.

**Time:** there are **10 school days** until the assembly.

**How the days work:** every day is a **full school day**: you go through all the places in order. If a teacher catches you, you lose an item and teachers become **more alert**, but the day goes on to the next place. The next morning you start again on the bus. You **keep your items and secrets** from day to day. You win when you reach your bike, on any day before the assembly. If you have not escaped after day 10, you have to sit through the assembly.

- **Type:** role-playing game (RPG)
- **Look:** 2D pixel art, or paper cut-out characters like the *Paper Mario* games
- **Plays in:** a web browser
- **Made by:** a teacher and a 14-year-old student
- **Played by:** MYP students

## Characters

At the start you pick one character. Each one is good at something different.

| Character | Good at |
|-----------|---------|
| The sporty one | Moves faster |
| The clever one | Notices the teachers' habits |
| The funny one | Tells a joke that distracts everybody |
| The quiet one | Makes less noise |

## The locations

The locations follow the order of a school day. The classroom comes back more than once.

| # | Location | Who is there |
|---|----------|--------------|
| 1 | School bus | Bus driver |
| 2 | Classroom (1st lesson) | Teacher |
| 3 | Outside at break time | Teacher on duty |
| 4 | Classroom (2nd lesson) | Teacher |
| 5 | Canteen at lunch | Teacher on duty |
| 6 | Classroom (3rd lesson) | Teacher |
| 7 | Car park, where your bike is | |

We took out the principal's office. The game is simpler without it.

## How you play

- **Collect items** that help you escape.
- **Talk to people** to learn secrets.
- **Swap** items or sweets with other students.
- **Sneak past teachers** without them seeing you.
- **No fighting.** You never fight anyone. You just get away.

## Sneaking

Sneaking is about **being quiet** and **not getting the teacher's attention**.

The best time to move is when the teacher is busy. For example, when they are:

- using their computer
- turned around, writing on the board

## Getting caught

There is **no game over**. If a teacher catches you:

1. You **lose items**.
2. The teachers become **more alert**. They **look at you more often**, so sneaking gets harder.

## Items

Items help you escape. Some are normal school things. Some are funny things you would not expect to find at school.

| Item | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| Helicopter hat | A hat with a helicopter rotor. It lets you fly. |
| Ladder | *(to decide)* |
| Hall pass | *(to decide)* |
| Special quiet shoes | Walk without making noise. |
| Sweets | Swap them with other students for items. |

## The escape plan: every stage has a job

The school day has **4 stages**. Each stage gives you a different part of your escape plan:

| Stage | Its job in the plan | What you get there |
|---|---|---|
| 🚌 **Bus** | **The getaway** | 🔑 bike key (needed for every escape), 🚁 helicopter hat |
| 📐 **Maths** | **The tools** | 🎫 hall pass, 🪜 folding ladder, 💨 whoopee cushion |
| 🍎 **Break** | **The ways out** | find the 3 ways out, 🔢 gate code, 🪣 toy spade |
| 🧪 **Science** | **Getting away unseen** | 🧪 smoke potion, 🔋 batteries, 🔦 torch |

There are **3 ways out**. Each one needs things from **different stages**:

| Way out | 📐 Maths | 🍎 Break | 🧪 Science | 🚌 Bus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🚪 **The main entrance**: show a hall pass, type the code, and make smoke so the office can't see you leave | 🎫 hall pass | 🔢 gate code | 🧪 smoke potion | |
| 🧱 **Over the high wall**: climb up with a ladder, fly down the other side | 🪜 ladder | | 🔋 batteries | 🚁 helicopter hat |
| 🕳️ **The hole under the hedge**: dig it bigger, make a rude noise so nobody hears, and light up the dark tunnel | 💨 whoopee cushion | 🪣 toy spade | 🔦 torch | |

Every escape also needs your 🔑 **bike key** from the bus.

The game shows which stage each item comes from (in the escape plan, press B).

## Secrets

Teachers have **habits**, and the habits change on **different days**. If you learn a habit, you know the best time to sneak.

Examples:

- A teacher drinks coffee at a certain time.
- A teacher opens the window if something happens.

The teachers on duty at break and lunch have habits too.

## Level 1: the school bus (built!)

The bus driver took your **bike key** yesterday because you sang too loudly. The key hangs next to him at the front of the bus.

- Sneak to the front, take the key, and be **sitting in a seat** when the bus arrives at school.
- Sitting in a seat hides you. Standing in the aisle means the driver can see you in his **mirror**.
- The driver checks his mirror again and again. Before he looks, a **?** appears. When he looks, a **!** appears and the aisle turns red.
- **Noise:** walking makes a little noise, running makes a lot. Too much noise makes the driver look.
- **Driver habits:** each weekday the driver does something that stops him looking in the mirror (singing, coffee, a giant sandwich). Students tell you these secrets. Secrets you know appear as green stripes on the bus bar.
- **Students have no names.** Students with a **speech bubble** know a secret or have something to swap.
- **Swaps:** 2 sweets → sunglasses (Leo), 1 sweet → helicopter hat (Zoe), sunglasses → quiet shoes (Quinn). Ben gives you a free sweet.

## Level 2: the classroom (built!)

A maths lesson with **Mr Grumble**. He uses his computer a lot.

- **Goal:** find **3 important things** before the bell rings, and be **at a desk** when it rings:
  - 🎫 the **hall pass**, on the cabinet next to Mr Grumble
  - 🪜 the **folding ladder**, in the cupboard at the back
  - 💨 the **whoopee cushion**, in the lost property box
- **Hiding:** at any **empty desk**, or by the **bookshelf**.
- **Mr Grumble** switches between his **computer** and **writing on the board** (both safe). Before he looks at the class, a **?** appears. When he looks, a **!** appears and the floor turns red.
- When teachers are more alert, Mr Grumble **walks around the room**. He sees everything 2 steps around him.
- **Students have no names.** Students with a **speech bubble** know a secret or have something to swap. You have to reach them without getting caught.
- **Mr Grumble's habits:** Monday slow computer, Tuesday giant equation, Wednesday leaves for coffee, Thursday his mum phones, Friday a video with the lights off.
- **Funny items:** the ✨ **magic equation** multiplies your sweets by 2 every morning (up to 10). **Cardboard you** sits in your place at the end of a level, so you don't need to be hiding (works once).
- You keep the things you find. If you get caught, the teacher takes one back and it goes back where you found it.

## Level 3: break time (built!)

Break time is for **exploring** and **making your escape plan**.

- The playground is **bigger than the screen**. The view moves with you.
- You can **walk anywhere** in the playground. The **striped areas are OUT OF BOUNDS**. If Ms Whistle sees you there, you are caught. You can hide in **bushes**.
- **Ms Whistle** (PE teacher, teacher on duty) **walks around** the playground. She only sees the area **around her** (the light circle).
- **Ms Whistle's habits:** Monday referees football, Tuesday chases seagulls, Wednesday eats lunch on the bench, Thursday times running laps, Friday on her phone.
- **3 ways out.** When you find one, it goes in your **escape plan** (press B) with the things you need. (See *The escape plan* above.)
- **Swap chain:** find the ⚽ football in the bushes → swap it for a 📚 comic → swap the comic for the 🔢 gate code. The little kid in the sandpit swaps the 🪣 toy spade for a sweet.
- **Break is short (50 seconds)**, so you can't explore everything in one day.

## Level 4: science (built!)

A science lesson with **Professor Fizz**, after break and before home time.

- **Professor Fizz never sits down.** She walks around the lab all lesson. She only sees what is **in front of her** (a cone of light), so you move **behind her back**.
- She **stops** at her bench (back to the class) and at the back of the lab (looking along it).
- If you make a **loud noise**, she **turns around** to look at you.
- **Hiding:** on any **empty stool**. Be on a stool when the bell rings.
- **Find 3 things:** 🧪 the **smoke potion** (chemicals shelf at the front), 🔋 the **batteries** (supply box at the back) and 🔦 the **torch** (hook at the back).
- 🧪 **Smoke potion:** you need it for the main entrance. You can also press **U** to use it now (nobody can see you for 6 seconds), but then it is gone until you find another one.
- A student in the lab swaps a 🚁 helicopter hat for 2 sweets (if you didn't get one on the bus).
- **Professor Fizz's habits:** Monday her experiment goes BOOM (smoke), Tuesday Gerald the frog escapes, Wednesday steamed-up goggles, Thursday electric hair, Friday a volcano.

## The Great Escape (final level, built!)

When the school day ends and your plan is ready (🔑 bike key + everything for one way out), the bell rings: **"Everybody to the hall for ASSEMBLY PRACTICE!"** This is your chance.

- The playground at the end of the day. Everybody else is inside, so **any student outside is suspicious**.
- **Three teachers search for you at the same time:**
  - **Mr Grumble** walks around the sandpit and the main entrance (sees in a small circle)
  - **Ms Whistle** walks around the playground (sees in a circle)
  - **Professor Fizz** walks along the garden and the high wall (sees in a cone in front of her)
- If **any** teacher sees you, you are caught. Hide in the **bushes**.
- At your way out, **follow your plan**: choose your things **in the right order** (press 1, 2, 3… or click).
  - 🚪 Main entrance: 🎫 hall pass → 🧪 smoke potion → 🔢 gate code
  - 🧱 High wall: 🪜 ladder → 🔋 batteries → 🚁 helicopter hat
  - 🕳️ Hedge hole: 💨 whoopee cushion → 🪣 toy spade → 🔦 torch
- The wrong thing doesn't help (and sometimes it's funny). Try again!
- Then you run to the car park, unlock your bike with your 🔑 key, and **ride away. YOU ESCAPED!**
- If you get caught, you go to assembly practice and lose a thing. Try again the next day.

## How to win

Collect your plan during the school days, then do **the great escape** before the end of day 10.
